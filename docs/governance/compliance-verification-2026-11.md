| 3 | §2 RA: open question whether automated or AI-generated analysis counts as research | S1 reg 2(1)(w), 2(1)(wa), 2(1)(u), 2(1)(fa); reg 19(vii); reg 24(7) | **unclear: ask counsel** (text points to "yes") | "Research report" means "any written or electronic communication" with research analysis or an opinion concerning securities (S1 2(1)(w)). ## 4. Questions for counsel (priority order)

1. **Broker association (16A).** We call Upstox APIs ("interaction of information technology systems", S3 16A Explanation 2(c)). What must AuraTrade not do externally so that Upstox, or any future broker or data partner, does not breach reg 16A? Is a SEBI-specified digital platform route relevant?
2. **Backtests and performance figures.** Are published backtest results, equity curves or "beats Nifty" statements an implied "claim of returns or performance" (S3 16A(1)(ii))? Can an unregistered tool show users backtests of *their own* rules? Is PaRRVA verification (S3 16E) the only route?
3. **AI interpretation as research.** Does per-security AI commentary to external users make AuraTrade a research analyst (S1 2(1)(u), (w), (wa))? Does a subscription for the software count as consideration for research services (S1 2(1)(fa))? Would RA registration or a partner RA be needed for G1?
4. **NSE data for simulation.** Does S14 §7.4 ("virtual trading or simulation") cover paper trading fed by broker or vendor data, for external users and for founder-only use? What NSE Data licence covers display and derived-data redistribution (S14 §7.3, S15 §3.5)?
5. **Screens as tools.** Is a user-configured screener or backtester outside the RA and IA Regulations? Do pre-built screens or shortlists become "trading calls" or "research analysis"?
6. **Human-approved orders.** For a future live phase: is an AuraTrade-proposed order, approved per order by the user and sent via a broker API, an algo order (S8, S9 Q8)? What does the founder's own use below 10 OPS require (static IP, generic algo ID)? Confirm with the broker too.
7. **IA "widely available to the public" proviso.** Does it cover a logged-in SaaS (S2 2(1)(l) first proviso)? Where is the line for portfolio analysis features?
8. **DPDP dates and relief.** Confirm the publication date (13 or 14 Nov 2025) and the Act section commencement notification. Is there any s.17(3) notification for startups? How do we reconcile one-year log retention (rules 6(1)(e), 8(3)) with erasure requests?
9. **Education positioning.** If any content is positioned as education, confirm the 30-day lag (S4, from 1 Jul 2026) and how its ban on naming a security with a price indication applies to a UI.
